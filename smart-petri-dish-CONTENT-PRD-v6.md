---

**Step 1**
- Number: 1
- Title: Add Your Sample
- Body: Place your sample in the correct compartment — water, sputum, or disease panel. The device is designed to be used at home, no training required.

**Step 2**
- Number: 2
- Title: Real-Time Analysis
- Body: Three integrated bio-sensors analyze your sample simultaneously. High-sensitivity detection identifies bacteria, contaminants, and disease markers in minutes.

**Step 3**
- Number: 3
- Title: Secure Transmission
- Body: Results are encrypted and transmitted to the cloud via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth — reaching our platform securely, wherever you are.

**Step 4**
- Number: 4
- Title: Doctor-Validated Report
- Body: A licensed provider reviews the AI analysis and delivers a validated report to your app — with clear next steps you can actually act on.

---

**Workflow mini-summary (3 nodes):**

Node 1 — Bio Sensors (x3)
- Highly sensitive detection
- Real-time, simultaneous analysis
- No lab equipment needed
- Low power consumption

Node 2 — Encrypted Transmission
- Secure via Wi-Fi / Bluetooth
- End-to-end encrypted
- Cloud-based processing

Node 3 — Your App + Doctor
- Clear SAFE / POSITIVE / NEGATIVE flags
- Doctor-written next steps
- Full history and trends

---

**FAQ — 3 accordion items:**

**Q: Do I need medical training to use the device?**
> No. The Smart Petri Dish is designed for everyday use at home or in your community.
> Simply add your sample to the labeled compartment — the device handles the rest.
> Our onboarding guide walks you through the full process in under 5 minutes.

**Q: How do I get my results?**
> Results are delivered to the Smart Petri Dish app on your phone or computer.
> You'll see a clear risk summary (SAFE / POSITIVE / NEGATIVE) for each test,
> your doctor's validated report, and recommended next steps — in plain language,
> not medical jargon.

**Q: Is my health data private and secure?**
> Absolutely. All data is encrypted end-to-end from the moment it leaves your device.
> Your health information is never sold or shared. Only you and your validated
> healthcare provider have access to your reports. Our platform is built with
> HIPAA-ready architecture for your protection.
