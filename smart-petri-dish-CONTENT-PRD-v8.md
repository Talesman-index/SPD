## ⚠️ CORE SYSTEM — v8.0 FINAL DEFINITION

> Smart Petri Dish is a **guided health screening system**
> that helps users take the right next step, with medical validation when needed.
>
> It is NOT:
> - A hospital replacement
> - A diagnosis tool
> - A complex telehealth platform
>
> **The 3 tests are:**
> 1. Water Safety Screening (pH, heavy metals, microbial presence)
> 2. Respiratory Sample Screening (sputum analysis, pattern detection)
> 3. Biological Risk Screening Panel (microbial activity, biomarkers)
>
> **Telehealth** = emergency only, not standard flow
> **Pharmacy** = after doctor validation only, not initial flow
> **Jobs section** = website only, not in the app

- Body: Your provider reviews the screening results and delivers a clear outcome to your app — with guidance on exactly what to do next.

---

**FAQ — 3 accordion items:**

**Q: Does the system decide what tests I take?**
> No. The system makes a recommendation based on your symptoms
> and profile — using a structured medical decision database,
> not guesswork. A licensed provider always reviews and validates
> that recommendation before you receive any instructions.

**Q: What happens after I get my results?**
> Your provider determines your next step. This could be:
> home monitoring, a pharmacy visit, scheduling a consultation,
> or a hospital referral. In urgent situations only, a telehealth
> link may be provided. The app guides you through whichever path applies.

**Q: Is my health information private?**
> Yes. All data is encrypted end-to-end from the moment you enter it.
> Your health information is never sold or shared. Only you and your
> assigned provider have access. Our platform is built with
> HIPAA-ready architecture.

- Title: Respiratory Sample Screening
- Subtitle: Mucus Analysis · Pattern Detection · Risk Indicators
- Body: Analyzes sputum samples to identify abnormal patterns, color changes, and biological activity associated with potential respiratory issues. Results are AI-assisted and reviewed by healthcare providers.

- Title: Biological Risk Screening Panel
- Subtitle: Microbial Activity · Biomarkers · Early Warning Signals
- Body: Screens biological samples for unusual microbial growth and biomarker signals using multi-modal sensing (visual, electrochemical, and colorimetric). Designed for early risk detection, not diagnosis.
- Link: Read More

- Body: No screening result reaches you without a licensed provider reviewing it first. They confirm the findings, add clinical context, and determine your next step.
