## ⚠️ CORE SYSTEM UPDATE — v7.0

> The Smart Petri Dish is not simply a device that sends results.
> It is a **guided medical triage system** that operates in 3 phases:
>
> **Phase 1 — Guided Intake**
> The user creates a profile, enters their medical history,
> and completes a symptom questionnaire inside the app.
>
> **Phase 2 — AI Pre-Recommendation (rule-based engine)**
> The app analyzes symptoms + history and automatically suggests
> which analyses to perform with the Petri Dish (urine, water, sputum, etc.).
> This recommendation is NOT executed automatically — it is forwarded to a doctor.
>
> **Phase 3 — Doctor Validation & Instructions**
> The doctor reviews the patient's history, symptoms, and AI recommendation
> via a dedicated dashboard. They can validate, modify, or reject it —
> and add supplementary instructions.
> Once validated, the patient receives step-by-step instructions on their app:
> which test to perform, how to do it, and what precautions to take.
>
> **Result:** Speed (automated pre-analysis) + Safety (human validation).
> The Petri Dish becomes an extension of the healthcare system —
> not a replacement for it.
